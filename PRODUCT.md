# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Internal teams that plan, organize, and supervise agent-driven work across projects. Users need to understand project structure, task state, ownership, and progress quickly while working inside the Kanban board.

## Product Purpose

Agent Kanban is an operational workspace for structuring projects and moving agent tasks through a shared workflow. Success means that teams can organize work into clear project-specific hierarchies, act on tasks without losing context, and monitor execution from one dependable surface.

## Brand Personality

Professional, simple, and modern. The interface should feel calm, precise, capable, and focused on the work rather than decorative presentation.

## Anti-references

Avoid ornamental SaaS-dashboard styling, excessive cards, decorative gradients, glass effects, and visual complexity that competes with the Kanban workflow. Do not turn hierarchy into a separate administrative experience disconnected from the board.

## Design Principles

- Keep project structure and task workflow visible in the same operational context.
- Make hierarchy understandable through clear labels, navigation, and progressive disclosure.
- Prefer direct manipulation and concise actions over configuration-heavy flows.
- Preserve status visibility and task momentum at every hierarchy level.
- Add polish through precision, responsiveness, and feedback rather than decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Maintain keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, sufficient text and UI contrast, status cues that do not rely on color alone, and reduced-motion alternatives for non-essential animation.
